# LOCAL-MONID — Monid integration workstream map

## Scope

Repository-local Monid discovery/search integration, installation records,
and future maintenance that is specific to that integration.

## Boundaries

Research performed with Monid belongs to the workstream that owns the research
question. Generic agent skills, unrelated search providers, and product
features do not belong here.
